About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Warwick. Before joining Warwick, I was with Yale University, hosted by Prof. Fan Zhang and Prof. Zhong Shao.

I earned my PhD at Imperial College London under the supervision of Prof. Arthur Gervais. Prior to that, I received my MSc from Imperial College London and my BE from Southeast University.

I co-founded Decentralized Intelligence (D23E), where we advance decentralization and intelligence.

Interested in working with me?

I am always looking for self-motivated students. If you are interested, please reach out with your CV and a brief explanation of why you are a good fit at kaihua[at]qin.ac.

Research Interests

Decentralized Systems

Securing blockchains and decentralized systems, focusing on information security, distributed computing, and applied cryptography.

Cryptoeconomics

Using game theory, mechanism design, and causal inference to align incentives and ensure resilient decentralized finance.

AI for Security

Applying AI to enhance cybersecurity, including automating threat detection and vulnerability management.

Secure Agentic Systems

Designing secure architectures, infrastructure, and protocols for dependable AI agent ecosystems.
